MYPENAn AI security-testing assistant that can only act inside a signed authorization. v0.1 shipped2026

MYPEN plans a security test, runs and chains proven tools, adapts to what it finds, and drafts the report. It doesn't invent new attacks.

What makes it safe to hand to an AI is one gate: every action is checked against the signed scope, and every decision is recorded. The model proposes, the gate decides, the record proves it. A made-up or out-of-scope target is refused and logged, never run.

It finds, confirms and reports; it doesn't exploit. For authorized engagements, labs and study only.

How I build

Three rules on every project.

Tested before it's trusted

Before an AI tool goes live, I try to break it: tricked instructions, leaked data, confident wrong answers. Then I keep checking after launch.

Data stays home

Personal data stays in Rwanda unless the law allows otherwise. AI sees only what it needs to do the job.

People sign off

Anything that moves money or judges a person waits for a human to approve it. Automation does the work; people make the call.
